# Three-minute demo outline

Use your own words and describe your actual work and assistance accurately. The app is ready to demonstrate; claims of interviews, adoption, or measured community impact require evidence you collect yourself.

## 0:00–0:25 — A local question

Show the default Dublin/Pleasanton BART origin. Explain the question: a transit stop nearby does not tell a person which everyday places they can reach within their available time. Mile uses real schedules and a walking network to make that time visible.

## 0:25–1:05 — A working journey

Keep the initial date and a 45-minute budget. Choose **Libraries**, then **Alameda County Library Dublin**. Open the itinerary and point to the walking, waiting, and Wheels ride. The exact result depends on the selected date/time and snapshot; read the actual displayed values rather than memorizing a fixed number.

Change the travel budget or transfer allowance and show that the result recalculates. Demonstrate that a trip's wait time counts against the budget.

## 1:05–1:40 — What the evening changes

Use **What changes after 8 pm?** or **Compare times**. Explain that the same origin, date, time budget, and walking allowance are used for 8 am and 8 pm. Point out the actual differences. Some categories may improve while others decline; avoid claiming every service gets worse at night.

## 1:40–2:10 — Why access matters

Toggle **Vehicle access** and click a Census tract. Show the no-vehicle household estimate, margin of error, and release year. Explain that these figures describe the whole tract, not a count of residents served by a highlighted path.

## 2:10–2:40 — The programming

Explain the parts you understand and can demonstrate:

- Combining two timetable sources with agency-specific IDs and date calendars.
- Searching the connected walking graph and scanning transit trips in boarding rounds.
- Counting waiting and transfer time and reconstructing an itinerary.
- Running analysis in a Web Worker so controls remain usable.
- Rejecting incompatible service periods and checking the shipped data checksum.

## 2:40–3:00 — Trust and limits

Open **Data & sources**. Show the actual sources and saved snapshot. Briefly state that the app measures scheduled access, not live delays or place opening hours. Export a CSV or copy a reproducible view link.
